Embroidery socks

A raised thread logo on the cuff or leg — the finish most buyers choose when the sock has to look like a retail product, not a giveaway.

Request a quote WhatsApp

In detail

The logo is sewn, so it reads as premium.

Embroidery is stitched after knitting on a dedicated machine. It gives a raised, slightly glossy mark with visible thread direction — closer to apparel branding than to print. Typical placement is the outer cuff or mid-leg; up to 8 colours of thread.

Questions buyers ask

Before you send artwork

Straight answers, no sales copy.

Where can the embroidery sit?

Outer cuff or mid-leg. Cuff placement survives the most washing.

How many thread colours are possible?

Up to 8 per logo position.

Which artwork file do you need?

AI, EPS, PDF or a 300 dpi PNG. If you only have a JPG we redraw it before sampling.

Can embroidery be combined with jacquard?

Yes. A knit-in body with an embroidered badge is a common club specification.
