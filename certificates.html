How to read them

What a buyer checks first

Who issued it

A certificate is only worth what the issuing institute is worth. OEKO-TEX reports come from the institute named on the scan - check that name, not just the logo.

The certificate number

OEKO-TEX numbers can be looked up through the issuing institute. Ours is printed on the report and in the filename.

The expiry date

OEKO-TEX certificates run for one year at a time and are renewed annually. Ask for the current one rather than an old copy.

The product class

Class I covers baby articles and is the strictest; Class II covers articles worn next to skin. Adult socks sit in Class II.

Questions buyers ask

About our certificates

Straight answers, no sales copy.

Does Mingzhi itself hold OEKO-TEX?

No, and that is normal. The OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certificates belong to our yarn supplier and our dyeing supplier. What is certified is the material that ends up against skin.

What does Class I mean?

Class I is the strictest product class - articles for babies and toddlers. Adult socks only need Class II, so a Class I certificate covers more than most orders require.

Which certificate is ours?

The CE / GPSD declaration and its six-page test report, issued to Jiaxing Mingzhi Textile.
