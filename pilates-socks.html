The line

The category is fashion-led. The construction still has to hold.

Pilates socks sell on how they look and how they feel on a reformer — but they are returned when the grip fails or the cuff rolls. Studios need both: a colour story that sells at the front desk, and a construction that survives studio washing.

We knit a thin, dense base for reformer use (no bunching under the foot), add grip in a layout that clears the arch, and finish the cuff so it stays put. Then we build the colour range around your studio.

Typical studio order: 300 pairs across 3–4 colourways, split across S/M/L, in your own packaging. Because the minimum is 100 pairs, you can open a colour without committing to a container; reorders run smaller and faster.

For studios

A studio programme, not just a sock order.

Studio retail lives on colour stories and repeat drops. We support both.

Colour stories

Build a 4–6 colour range around your studio interior. We match Pantone on yarn, and keep the recipe for reorders.

Drops, not batches

Hold the construction, change the colourway. Call off 200–1,000 pairs per drop across the year instead of one large buy.

Private label

Your label, hang tag, belly band and polybag. Retail-ready, barcoded if your POS needs it.
