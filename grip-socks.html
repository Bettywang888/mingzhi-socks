Grip socks

Silicone grip on the sole for Pilates, barre, trampoline and indoor training — in three processes, each with a different feel underfoot.

Request a quote WhatsApp

In detail

Three grip processes, one sole.

Silicone print gives the sharpest detail and the best wet grip. PVC is the cheapest per pair and the most hard-wearing. Flocked grip is softer and quieter on studio floors. Layout can be full sole, heel-to-toe strips or a repeating dot pattern.

Questions buyers ask

Before you send artwork

Straight answers, no sales copy.

Which grip materials do you offer?

Silicone, PVC or flocked. Silicone is the most durable and the most common for Pilates.

Full sole or dots?

Either. Full-sole, heel-to-toe strips or a repeating dot layout.

Does grip change the minimum order?

No. 100 pairs per style per colour, the same as any other construction.

Can grip go on a printed sock?

Yes. The sock is knitted and printed first, then grip is applied.
