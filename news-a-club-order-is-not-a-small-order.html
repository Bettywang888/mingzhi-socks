A club order is not a small order

The most common misread in this trade is that 60 pairs is a small order. It is a small total and a complicated order, and that gap explains most quotes that come back higher than a club expected.

Take two orders of 600 pairs. The first is one design, one colourway, one size split. The second is a club: two colourways, six sizes, names on the leg, and packing per player. Same total, and the second takes a completely different shape of week.

Four things drive it, and only the first is about yarn.

Setup is per programme, not per pair. A colourway change means re-threading and re-programming. Two colourways is two setups before the first sock exists.

Size splits create short runs. Six sizes means six short runs, and the smallest may be a few dozen pairs. The machine is set for each changeover regardless.

Names are a programme each. A name knitted into the sock is a jacquard programme per name — twenty players is twenty programmes before a pair is made. Printing or sublimating the names avoids that at low volume, at the cost of a different surface under the finger. Ask which method a quote assumes, because the two behave nothing alike.

Packing per player multiplies handling. Sixty pairs in sixty individual bags with a name on each is sixty small decisions, and this is the step clubs underestimate most.

None of that makes a club order a bad order. It means the honest way to buy one is to simplify what can be simplified before asking for a price: two colourways instead of four, three sizes instead of six, names only if the club will genuinely use them, and packing in tens unless the socks are handed out one by one.

There is also a floor, and it is worth saying plainly. Below 100 pairs per colourway a knitting mill is not the right supplier, and negotiation does not change the setup maths. If you need two pairs, or ten, the honest answer is a print-on-demand service printing onto a blank sock — different product, different economics, correct tool for that job. We would rather say so than quote something we cannot run.

What we do is 100 pairs per style per colour, with a size split you choose, and grip, knitted colour or embroidery in the same order. Send the colourways and the split and we will tell you which parts are actually driving the price.
