The line

A performance sock your buyer can feel in the hand.

Most private-label sport socks look right on a shelf and fail after ten washes: the cuff slackens, the heel thins, the toe seam rubs. The difference is not the design — it is the yarn blend, the knitting density and where the reinforcement sits.

We build from your target retail price backwards: choose the yarn (combed cotton, polyester, nylon blends, merino), the density, the cushioning zones, then the decoration. You get a construction sheet with the sample, so a repeat order matches the first one.

Decoration options: knit-in jacquard, terry logo, flat-knit logo, silicone print, woven label, embroidery on cuff.
